How to install

Do both: the PC app and the phone app. Pick the device you're on.

Swipe sideways to see all 7 steps →

1Download

On your phone, tap the green Android phone button above. Chrome warns “File might be harmful” — tap Download anyway.

2Open it

When “File downloaded” appears at the top, tap Open.

3Allow once first time only

If your phone says it isn't allowed to install unknown apps, tap Settings.

4Install

Turn on Allow from this source, then tap Install.

5Quick scan sometimes

Google Play Protect may suggest a scan. Tap Scan app — it takes a few seconds.

6Looks safe

“This app looks safe.” Tap Install.

7Done

“App installed.” Tap Open. PocketPad is now on your home screen too.

Screenshots are from a realme phone. Other brands word things slightly differently, but the steps are the same. PocketPad isn't on the Play Store yet, which is why Android shows these warnings.

  1. Download with the blue Windows PC button above, then open PocketPad-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. “Windows protected your PC”? Click More info then Run anyway. It shows because the app is new, not because something is wrong.
  3. Click Yes when Windows asks for permission, then Install and Finish.
  4. That's it. The controller driver and firewall setting are installed for you. Nothing else to download.
  5. Open PocketPad from your desktop (click Yes again). Keep its window open while you play.
Every time you play

Connect & play

Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi. After the first time, it's just open both apps and tap Connect.

1

Open the PC app

Double-click PocketPad on your desktop. It shows a QR code.

2

Scan with the phone

In the phone app, tap the QR button next to Connect and point it at the PC screen.

3

Start your game

The PC window turns green. Now start the game — it sees a normal Xbox controller.

Help

Something not working?

“Couldn't find the PC”

Check the PC app is open and both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, then scan the QR code again. If your PC moved to a different Wi-Fi, the phone looks for it by itself (“Looking for your PC…”).

It never connects on hotel, café, airport or office guest Wi-Fi

Those networks stop devices from talking to each other. Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it instead.

The game doesn't react

Connect first, then start the game. If the game was already running, restart it.

It disconnects by itself

On the phone: Settings → Apps → PocketPad → Battery → Unrestricted. Some phones put apps to sleep to save battery.

The delay number is orange or red

Move closer to the Wi-Fi router, or use the phone's hotspot.

Can I play with friends?

Yes — up to 4 phones on one PC. Everyone installs the phone app and scans the same QR code; each phone becomes its own controller.

How do I update or remove it?

To update, download from the buttons again and install over the old version — your settings stay. To remove: on the PC, Settings → Apps → PocketPad → Uninstall; on the phone, hold the icon → Uninstall.
